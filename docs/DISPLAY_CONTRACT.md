# GlucoCraft display contract

This document defines how GlucoCraft's values reach the display card and the wall panels: what GlucoCraft delivers,
how each display shows it, and the rules a display applies itself. Works are cited in APA 7 author–date form. Full
references: Documentation > References.

**One system:** GlucoCraft (the integration) decides every value, word, color and direction. The display card
(`www/glucocraft-display-card.js`) and the wall panels (`glucocraft-panel.yaml` and the panels generated from it,
with `glucocraft_panel_binary.h` and `gc_units.h`) only show them. A display does its own
work only where it needs its own clock or must keep working while Home Assistant cannot reach it (the **clock rules**
below), and for the graph layout. Every comparison of the display card with the panels is checked against this page.

## Gap rule

One rule for every graph, the statistics and *sensor active*: a gap is an interval between two readings longer than
max(15 minutes, 3 × the reading interval). GlucoCraft rule; no consensus document defines a data gap.

- **Per stretch, on every surface:** the reading interval at each reading is the median (upper middle) of the 10
  intervals before it, filled with the intervals after the judged one when fewer than 10 come before; the judged
  interval itself never counts, nor do intervals of 0 s. GlucoCraft (statistics, sensor active, report:
  `gaps.py` `local_intervals` / `gap_threshold`), the display card and the dashboard graphs (`gcGapThrAt` /
  `gcGaps`, once in the cards' shared module `glucocraft-shared.js`) and the wall panels (`gc_units.h` `gcu::is_gap`,
  on the readings they hold: the files' readings and the 6-hour ring; no change to the file format) all compute it
  this way.
- **Fallback, with fewer than 3 intervals around one interval** (it differs per surface):
  - GlucoCraft: the detected interval of the stored readings (300 s while none is known);
  - the display card and the dashboard graphs: `gap_threshold_seconds` of `sensor.glucocraft_configuration` (the
    source in use); without it, max(900 s, 3 × `reading_interval_seconds`, or the card's own interval);
  - the wall panels: the 15-minute floor alone (they have no detected interval).
- `sensor.glucocraft_configuration` publishes `reading_interval_seconds` and `gap_threshold_seconds` for the source
  in use; for the cards they are the fallback, not the rule.
- **Why:** 15 minutes, because some CGM systems deliver their history in 15-minute steps (for example after a
  reconnection) and cloud delivery has short synchronization dropouts; 3 intervals, so that two missed readings of a
  slower source are not yet a gap; per stretch, from the readings around each interval, so that the rule adapts when 1-minute readings
  follow 15-minute history or a second source has another interval; one rule, so that the graphs, the statistics and
  *sensor active* describe the same data.

## Pages

17 pages on the display card and every panel. The code numbers them 0–16; the card shows the user "page N of 17"
(the index + 1): indexes 0–6 the graphs (6 hours to 6 months; pages 1–7 of 17), 7–15 the nine weekly pages (pages
8–16 of 17), and the About page:

- **Index 16, About GlucoCraft** (page 17 of 17): GlucoCraft's icon, its disclaimer in its short form (const.py
  DISCLAIMER_SHORT, its first sentence as the title; the long form is in the Documentation), the version line and, under it, the copyright line "Copyright (C) 2026 The
  GlucoCraft authors. License: gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0" (two lines, in the version line's size; AGPL-3.0 section 0,
  "Appropriate Legal Notices"), word for word as on the panels.

It fits at 14 px or more on every card and in the cards' compact layout; the cards' text descriptions read it as its
texts. The panels also show a start-up screen: GlucoCraft's icon, its name, the panel's firmware version and the line
"For development and testing only. Not for medical use."; it shows for 15 seconds at every start and needs no input

The display cards have no start-up screen. The
wording is GlucoCraft's (`LANGUAGE_STYLE.md` rule 14): the displays show values; they never describe them as
accurate or reliable.

## Clock rules (the displays' own work)

| Item | Rule | Why on the display |

| Clock override | if the age passes `binary_sensor.glucocraft_no_current_data` `threshold_minutes` (default 15, ≤ 0 → 15; the last known limit is kept while the entity is unavailable): no current data, as if the flag were on (the words and their throb), and while the status is not warm_up / expired the status bar red | Home Assistant unreachable: GlucoCraft's own flag and status cannot arrive |

## Accessibility of the display cards (`ACCESSIBILITY.md` rules 4 and 5)

- **Text description:** the drawing carries a description (`role="img"`, `aria-label`) in the rule-4 order: the data
  status only when there is a problem ("Sensor expired." after the stated expiry while readings still arrive), the
  value with its unit written out and the zone word ("Glucose 62 milligrams per deciliter, low."; LO
  / HI: "below the sensor's lower limit"), the time of the reading ("Measured 5 minutes ago."), the trend ("Changing
  slowly, minus 3 milligrams per deciliter in 5 minutes."), then one sentence on the page shown ("6-hour graph: lowest
  58, highest 160 milligrams per deciliter." / "Time in range of each 14-day period, last 26 weeks: from 81.0 to 93.0
  percent."); a comparison sign of a weekly page's description in words ("Consensus at most 36 percent").
  Without a current reading the screen's order: the same words as in the value's place first, as a sentence
  ("Sensor warming up." / "Sensor expired." / "No current data."), then the last reading with when it was taken
  and its age ("Last reading 64 milligrams per deciliter, today at 5:39 PM, 2 hours ago."; what is not known is left
  out), then the page sentence: no zone word and no trend, as on the screen; the throb changes nothing in it. At the
  sensor's limit: "Glucose below the sensor's lower limit, very low.", since when and the last measured value, and no
  trend, as on the LO / HI screen. While a GlucoCraft Repairs issue is open, "Check Home Assistant." before the page
  sentence. The About
  page (*About GlucoCraft*) names the drawing by its title, and its text follows as text, after the drawing, so that it
  can be read word by word.
- **Page changes:** a page chosen with a button or a swipe is announced through a live region that exists, empty,
  from the start: "Page 2 of 17: 24 Hours", then the page's sentence; the focus stays on the button.
- **Spoken summary:** `sensor.glucocraft_spoken_summary` (disabled by default) holds a shorter form of the description as
  one sentence for voice assistants, built by `spoken.py` with the same words and in the same order: the data status, the value with its unit and zone, the time of the reading and the trend word, without the 5-minute delta and the page sentence (during a warm-up only "Sensor warming up."): "Glucose 112
  milligrams per deciliter, tight range, measured at 7:46 AM, changing slowly." / "No current data. Last reading at 7:46
  AM." It is made again with each reading and on a change of the status, the no-current-data flag, the zone, the trend, the limit flag, the
  display unit, the decimal separator or the time or date format, and at midnight; never on the minute tick alone, so
  it names the time of the reading (the time format setting), never an age; a reading of an earlier day with its day ("Last reading yesterday at 9:45 PM.", or its
  date in the date format setting; `ENTITIES_AND_API.md`).
- **Spoken units:** one shared set of functions (`gcSpeak`, `gcSpeakA1c`, `gcSpeakAge`, `gcSpeakTrend`,
  `gcSpeakLimit`, `gcSpeakText`) in `glucocraft-shared.js`, the cards' shared module, following the display unit, the
  A1C / GMI unit and the decimal separator.
- **Keyboard:** real buttons over the drawn page buttons ("Previous page: About GlucoCraft", "Next page: 24 hours") and
  over the glucose block ("Open the weekly pages" / "Back to the 6-hour graph", offered only when the weekly pages can
  open); reached with Tab, used with Enter or Space, outlined when focused (the glucose block's ring inside it, as the
  block spans the card's width); the glucose block comes first, as it is drawn above the page buttons; they take no
  pointer events, so a click or a swipe works as before. The card returns to its 6-hour page after 3 minutes without
  use, not while one of its buttons has the focus or the pointer is over it; the card option *Return to the first page
  after 3 minutes* (on by default) turns this off.
- **Narrow screens:** below its design width a card does not shrink its
  drawing; it draws a compact layout at the width it has (the drawing's box, inside the card's border), one pixel per pixel, its parts stacked in the panel's order: header,
  value, zone word, the trend (arrow or word, 26 px at most) and 5-minute delta, then on the square card the status bar and the page, on the
  rectangular card the page and the status bar (as on the 800 x 480 panel, where it is the bottom strip); the page is the graph and statistics in a grid of 14 px
  labels, the weekly chart, or the About text; then the page buttons. The status bar is never above the glucose block. No text is below 14 px (the 12 px statistics
  labels remain only in the design layout). Checked at 360 and 412 px. The status bar's middle text ("Custom
  ranges", the warm-up, "Check Home Assistant") is centered when it clears both side texts by 12 px, otherwise centered
  in the room between them, and left out only without that room. On the start page the display card keeps its design
  width wherever the screen allows: it and the pages card stack instead of shrinking side by side, so the compact
  layout is drawn only on a screen narrower than the card.
- **One size:** the card keeps one size on every page and in every state, as the panels do: the square card
  480 x 480 and the rectangular card 800 x 480, each with its row of page buttons under it (56 px); the About page
  is drawn inside that frame with the panel's layout and text sizes. The compact layout has one frame for its width:
  the glucose block one height in every state (the value's room holds the value and its zone word, or the words
  with the last reading), every page drawn in the room of the tallest, the page buttons at the bottom.

## Not on the displays

No projection of future glucose values (a straight-line extrapolation is not a prediction; GlucoCraft makes no
projections); no verdict words (`LANGUAGE_STYLE.md` rule 2); no percentage of the period with data (*sensor active*:
the dashboard only); no Glycemia Risk Index (GRI): GlucoCraft calculates it for periods of 14 days and longer and shows it on
the dashboard and in the CGM Report only.
