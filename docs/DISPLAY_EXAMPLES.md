# Display Examples from Testers

> **For development and testing only.** GlucoCraft is free, open-source software, made available for development and
> testing. It is not intended for any medical purpose, such as diagnosing, monitoring or managing diabetes or making
> treatment decisions, and has not been cleared or approved by the FDA or any other regulator. Its information and
> notifications can be late, missing or wrong. Its notifications are not alarms. GlucoCraft is licensed under the
> AGPL-3.0-only, without warranty or, as far as the law allows, liability.

**What belongs here.** This page and its submissions are about testing GlucoCraft, developing it and displaying its
data. Diagnosing, treating or managing diabetes, treatment decisions and personal health information are outside this
project's topics; submissions and posts about them are closed.

Testers have built their own displays for GlucoCraft's data. This page lists them, each with a link and a short
description of what it shows. The descriptions are written by the GlucoCraft authors.

Each display is made, published and maintained by its author. It is not part of GlucoCraft and is not tested or
reviewed by the GlucoCraft project.

To have a display listed, open a *Share a display* issue ([CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md), *Sharing a display*).

| Display | What it shows | Made by | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
